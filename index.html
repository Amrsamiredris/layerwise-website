Questions

Before you book

Where does our data go?

Only to the AI providers and regions you approve in the Audit. Confidential and personal data can stay inside your own cloud account when the project needs it.

Do we have to replace our systems?

No. The whole approach is to add AI on top of what you run today. We only recommend a replacement when a system blocks the work, and we tell you why.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the code, the prompts, the workflows and the data. It lives in your accounts, and handover is documented so another team can run it.

How fast will we see results?

The Audit takes 2 to 3 weeks. A first integration usually goes live within 6 to 12 weeks, depending on access to your systems and approvals on your side.

What should AI not do in our company?

Make final decisions about people, money or customers without a person approving. We write those limits into your usage policy and into the agents themselves.
