Why most AI projects stall, and what we do instead.

Most UAE companies already run on tools they trust: a CRM, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business, an ERP. Their teams already use AI too, usually by pasting client data into chatbots with no rules about what goes where. Layerwise works the other way round. We start from the systems you have, find where the hours go, and add AI inside those tools to draft, sort, answer from your files and report. A person approves anything that reaches a customer, moves money or changes a record. Nothing gets replaced, and you own what we build.

Before you book

Where does our data go? Only to the AI providers and regions you approve in the Audit. Confidential and personal data can stay inside your own cloud account.

Do we have to replace our systems? No. We add AI on top of what you run today, and only suggest replacing a tool when it blocks the work.

Who owns what you build? You do: the code, the prompts, the workflows and the data. Handover is documented so another team can run it.

How fast will we see results? The Audit takes 2 to 3 weeks. A first integration usually goes live 6 to 12 weeks later, depending on access to your systems.

What will AI never do on its own? Make final decisions about people, money or customers. Those limits go into your staff policy and into the agents.

Do you work in Arabic? Yes. Meetings, documents and the AI's drafts can be in Arabic, English or both.

Which tools do you work with? Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Odoo and most tools with an API. The Audit confirms what yours allow.

Is the demo on this page real AI? No. It is a simple demo in your browser that shows the approval flow. Real projects use the models and rules agreed in the Audit.

We are a large company. Where do we start? With the same Audit, usually alongside a Fractional AI Lead who owns the program for you.
