Questions

Before you ask

Is this approved by Meta?

Layerwise Chat uses Meta's official Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp Cloud APIs — no passwords, no scraping. Your account must be an Instagram Professional account (Business or Creator).

Can I message anyone, anytime?

No tool can. Meta allows free-form messages within 24 hours of a person's last message. Broadcasts automatically skip people outside that window. WhatsApp messages outside the window need approved templates.

How fast can I go live?

Web chat and Telegram: same day. Instagram and Messenger: usually 1–3 days. WhatsApp depends on Meta's business verification.

What happens to my contacts if I leave?

You export them as CSV at any time. They're your contacts.

Do you support Arabic?

Yes — flows, keyword matching (Arabic and English keywords on the same trigger), AI replies and the web chat widget all work in Arabic.
